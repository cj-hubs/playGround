Terms & Conditions

Clear trip and billing guidelines for a smooth travel experience.

1. Rental Time Calculation

  • Rental time starts when the vehicle leaves the agreed starting point.
  • Rental time ends when the vehicle returns to the agreed return point.
  • The rental duration is calculated from the departure time to the return time.

2. Outstation Trip Charges

  • Outstation trips are charged based on the total kilometres travelled.
  • A minimum billing of 250 kilometres per day applies, even if the vehicle travels fewer than 250 kilometres.
  • If the vehicle travels more than 250 kilometres in a day, charges are calculated based on the actual kilometres travelled.
  • The applicable per-kilometre rate will be communicated at the time of booking.

3. Additional Charges

  • Driver allowance, toll charges, parking fees, and other applicable expenses may be charged separately.
  • Additional charges will be communicated in advance wherever applicable.

4. Customer Responsibility

  • Customers should return the vehicle to the agreed return point.
  • Any changes to the trip schedule or destination should be communicated to JKM Tours & Travels in advance.
